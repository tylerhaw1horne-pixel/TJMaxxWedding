OUR STORY

They knew each other for years.
Not closely. Not warmly.
Just names attached to family gatherings.

They did not expect fate to intervene
at Highlands —
a bad date, a bold interruption,
a drink that lasted too long.

Love did not arrive gently.
It arrived overwhelming.
She felt it first — visiting him at work,
a force too heavy to ignore.

He felt it when she refused to leave
during a season when leaving would have been easier.

Their past lives lingered.
Their love consumed them anyway.

On December 4th, 2025,
Jasper was born.
And suddenly the world shifted.

Learning each other.
Learning him.
Working together.

They became locked.
Obsessed.
Still are.

THE PROMISE

The first proposal was spontaneous.

The second was deliberate.
Grand Canyon.
December 8th, 2024.
Her home state.

Two promises.
Same devotion.
