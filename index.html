RECONNECTION LOG

They had known of each other for years.
Through family. Through proximity.
There was no interest. No attachment.
If anything — indifference.

Time passed.
They became different people.

When they met again, it wasn’t dramatic.
It wasn’t planned.

It was easy.

Conversation lasted longer than expected.
Neither of them forced it.

Something that never existed before
was suddenly obvious.
